A fuller Store for Seaglass Store Edition, on Seaglass 1.9.1: a new source, a front page like a store, the Store in big picture, Steam wishlist import and completion times for your whole library.

**The store** (*Settings → Experimental → Store*, off until you turn it on)

- **ElAmigos as a source.** Off until you choose it in *Settings → Experimental*. Seaglass reads its catalog and release pages and groups them with the other sources. ElAmigos offers its files through file hosts, so its releases open in your browser: Seaglass doesn't download or install them. Announcements are marked *Announced*, and patches listed on a release page don't count as its version. KaOs isn't included: its site refused access (HTTP 403) when checked.
- **A front page like a store.** Featured games at the top, artwork shelves, and recommendations from the games you played lately and your wishlist, saying what they're based on (Steam's popular games when there's nothing to go on). Games you have installed are left out. Tabs for new, popular and recently updated releases show a compact list with a preview of the selected game. Lists show the source, availability, version, Steam reviews and HowLongToBeat time where known; a source's publication date is never shown as the game's release date.
- **The Store in big picture.** Home, Browse with search on the on-screen keyboard, Wishlist, game pages, the choice of release and the install confirmation, all with the controller. Going back returns to the game you came from.
- **Import your Steam wishlist.** *Store → Wishlist → Import from Steam* fills in the Steam account on this PC (or enter another SteamID64). Games are matched by AppID and nothing you saved is removed. A private profile says so. Games without a known release stay on the wishlist, and Seaglass looks for them on your sources while the PC is idle.
- **Indexing keeps going.** Older releases are indexed while the PC is idle, five pages at a time, at least two seconds apart per source, and progress is saved after every page. A source that asks Seaglass to wait is left alone until then. Indexing pauses while you play, and *Pause indexing* on the Store page holds it until you resume.
- **Downloads explain a stopped engine.** When qBittorrent can't start, Downloads (in desktop mode and big picture) shows why and what to do, and lists the queued downloads that start once it runs.

**Your library**

- **How long to beat, for every game.** Game details and big picture's game sheet show HowLongToBeat's Main Story, Main + Extras and Completionist times for your installed games, from any store, also with the Store off. *Wrong game?* picks the right match, and the choice is shared with the Store.

This is experimental: expect rough edges. Big picture can't attach a .torrent file to a release; do that in desktop mode.
