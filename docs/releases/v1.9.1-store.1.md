Store discovery for Seaglass Store Edition, on Seaglass 1.9.1.

**The store** (*Settings → Experimental → Store*, off until you turn it on)

- **A Store that fills itself.** Turning the Store on asks once which repack sources to use (FitGirl, DODI). Seaglass indexes their releases in the background, catches up on older pages, and groups releases of the same game. No feed is needed.
- **Home, Browse and search.** Home shows new repacks, popular games (by the Steam chart), recently updated releases and wishlist activity. Browse filters and sorts everything that's indexed. Search answers from the index at once and then asks the sources.
- **Game pages.** Each game shows its releases, Steam reviews (overall and the last 30 days), the Metacritic score and HowLongToBeat times, with links to each. *Wrong game?* lets you pick the right match; DLC and mods are labelled.
- **Wishlist.** Add games and see when a new release turns up.
- **Install from a release.** *Download* on a release prepares it, attaches its torrent and opens the usual install confirmation with its safety checks. A release that is already installed offers *Install over it*.
- **Works with qBittorrent 5.2.** Downloads no longer stay queued with current qBittorrent versions (5.2 answers a login differently than 5.1).
- Fixed: Seaglass could stop responding after the first search that asked the sources.

**From Seaglass 1.9.1**

- The test harness and tests no longer touch your real data folder or log. This goes with Syncer 0.15.0, which brings back a Seaglass data folder that was emptied.

This is experimental: expect rough edges. Big picture keeps its Downloads screen and has no Store.
