The first **Seaglass Store Edition**: Seaglass 1.9.0 with an experimental store. It installs over Seaglass and keeps your library and settings; Seaglass installs back over it. From here on it updates itself from this repository, and it picks up Seaglass's own changes every day.

**The store** (*Settings → Experimental → Store*, off until you turn it on)

- **Catalogs from feeds you add.** The edition comes with none. Feeds are JSON files on the web ([docs/store-feed.md](https://github.com/ApolloF/Seaglass-Store/blob/main/docs/store-feed.md)), checked item by item and fetched every six hours. Games are grouped across feeds and get art and descriptions from the same sources as your library.
- **A recommended version.** It's the newest, unless another one has your language, comes from a feed you trust more, or the newest one's feed had downloads blocked. The page says why.
- **Background downloads through qBittorrent.** Seaglass runs your installed qBittorrent 5 hidden, with settings and torrents of its own, only while there's something to download. You can bind it to a network interface (a VPN: nothing moves while it's down), and set the port and UPnP, a proxy, encryption, DHT, PeX and LSD, speed limits and sharing. Downloads pause while you play, and from the tray.
- **Safety checks before anything runs.**
  - the installer against the feed's checksum
  - its publisher signature
  - what's in the download
  - a Microsoft Defender scan
  - optionally VirusTotal, by hash with your own key

  Problems warn or block; a blocked download installs only if you type its name. You can also open a download in Windows Sandbox first.
- **Installs without the installer's questions.** Inno Setup (with the language you picked), NSIS, Windows Installer, archives and ready-to-run games. The game's folder joins your library, and uninstalling works from Downloads.
- **Updates.** A newer version of a game the store installed shows as *Update*, and installs over the old one.
- **Big picture:** a read-only Downloads screen in Quick access.

This is experimental: expect rough edges, and read [what the safety checks can't do](https://github.com/ApolloF/Seaglass-Store/blob/main/docs/experimental-store.md#what-the-checks-cant-do).
